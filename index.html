About

Hello! I’m Christopher, a Product Owner based in Moss, Norway. After several years abroad, I’ve returned to Norway and am building a long-term career here.

At Euronext Securities, I’m part of a transformation programme harmonising business processes and digital services across five European markets. I focus on client master data and securities account administration, translating business, user, and regulatory needs into clear requirements and functional solutions.

Previously, I've had product owner responsibilities for the MyVPS web application, used by banks and financial institutions across the Nordics and Europe. I’ve also worked in client management and financial operations, and completed Le Wagon’s part-time web development course to deepen my understanding of software, databases, and APIs.

I enjoy the space where business understanding, technology, and collaboration meet—and helping teams turn a shared direction into dependable delivery.
